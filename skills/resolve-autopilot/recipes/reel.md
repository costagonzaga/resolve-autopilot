# Recipe: vertical reel (25–45 s)

- Timeline 1080x1920 (or 2160x3840), frame rate of the takes.
- Average clip 2–5 s; impact reels may cut under 1 s.
- Look: alternating zoom 1.0 / 1.12, warm CDL grade (defaults of `look`).
- Audio: voice -16 LUFS before the mix, music ducked under speech, SFX on cuts (skip flash cuts under 1 s),
  final -14 LUFS.
- Render: `--size 1080 1920`.
